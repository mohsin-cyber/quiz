2

Every swipe teaches you something. The app learns what you actually watch and finds you better lessons.

3

Teach what you know

Record a 60-second explainer of a topic you're good at. Help thousands of students — and get noticed.

Clean by design

Instagram and TikTok are built to keep you entertained. Connects is built to keep you learning — in a space you'd be happy to show your parents and teachers.

  • AI checks every video — it looks at the frames, listens to the audio and reads the caption before anything goes live.
  • Off-topic or vulgar? It never reaches the feed. Unclear cases go to a human moderator.
  • Report in one tap. Several reports hide a video until it's reviewed.
  • Block anyone, and delete your account and data whenever you want.

Questions

Is Connects free?

Yes. Watching, posting and learning are free for every student.

Who can join?

University students across Pakistan. A university email gives you the verified tick, but any email or Google account can join.

What can I post?

Anything that helps students learn: explainers, study and exam tips, skills, careers, internships and clean campus life. Videos up to 60 seconds.

When is the phone app coming?

Android and iPhone apps are in testing. Until then, use the full app on the web — it works on your phone's browser too.

How do I delete my account?

Inside the app: Profile → Delete account. Or read how account deletion works.
