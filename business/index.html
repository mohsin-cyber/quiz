Questions clients ask

How much does an app or website cost?

It depends on what it needs to do. After a short call we send a written quote in PKR, or USD for clients abroad, so you know the full price before anything starts. We usually split payment into parts tied to milestones.

How long does it take?

A logo or a set of social posts usually takes a few days. A business website usually takes one to three weeks, and an app usually takes one to three months. Your quote shows the exact dates.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. We work over WhatsApp, email and video calls, and we can quote and invoice in USD. Your portal works the same from anywhere.

Who owns the work when it is finished?

You do. At launch we hand over the source code, design files, and every login for your domain, hosting and app store accounts.

How do I log in to the client portal?

When your project starts we add your email. Open the client portal, type that email, then type the code we send you. There is no password to remember.

Can you fix or redesign my existing app or website?

Yes. Send us the link and tell us what is not working. We will tell you honestly whether fixing it or rebuilding it costs less.

We reply by email or WhatsApp with questions or a quote. No spam, no sales calls you did not ask for.
