Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

What is Connects University?

Connects University is a student-focused education and campus community platform built in Pakistan. It brings learning resources, university discovery, verified student profiles, teachers, mentors and campus conversations together in one place.

Is Connects University free for students?

Yes. Connects University is committed to keeping its core education and student community experience free for students. The mission is to reduce barriers to learning and connection.

Who founded Connects University?

Connects University was founded by Mohsin Haider Shakeel, a Karachi-based founder and digital creator focused on making education and campus opportunities easier to access for Pakistani students.

Who mentors Connects University?

Raza Abbas mentors Connects University with strategic guidance shaped by experience in project management, supply chain, professional training, consulting, leadership and career development. He first taught founder Mohsin Haider Shakeel Project Management at Iqra University and later helped him strengthen professional connections and the platform's reach.

Who can join Connects University?

The platform is designed for Grade 11 and 12 students, university students, teachers, mentors and university representatives. Account information and verification may differ by role.

What is Study Vault?

Study Vault is the learning library inside Connects University. It is designed to organize notes, books, past papers and trusted study material for Grade 11 and Grade 12 students by board, grade and subject.

Which education boards does Study Vault support?

Study Vault is designed to organize resources for Pakistani education boards, including Sindh, Aga Khan and Federal boards. Available material depends on verified contributions and will expand during beta.

Can teachers and mentors join?

Yes. Teachers can create professional mentor profiles, contribute learning resources and, after verification, connect with learners who need academic guidance.

How can universities participate?

Universities and their verified communities can share authentic campus life, programs and student experiences so prospective students can make better-informed education decisions.

How does account verification work?

Connects University uses role and institution information to distinguish authentic students, teachers and representatives. Verification features are being expanded carefully during beta.

Is Connects University in beta?

Yes. Connects University is currently in beta, so features are being tested and improved with feedback from students, teachers and institutions.
