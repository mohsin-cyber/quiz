Founder story

Building a more connected student journey

Mohsin founded Connects University around a simple idea: students should not have to search across disconnected places for learning material, authentic university information and a trusted campus community.

The platform begins with resources for Grade 11 and Grade 12 learners through Study Vault and continues into university discovery, verified student communities, teacher and mentor participation, and student opportunity features. Connects University is currently in beta and is being improved with real community feedback.

Its long-term mission is to make education access easier and help students remain connected throughout their academic journey.
