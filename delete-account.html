Delete your Connects University account

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Open the Connects University app or go to connects.university and sign in.
  2. Open My profile.
  3. Under Profile & account settings, tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account and sign-in are deleted straight away, and your uploaded files are queued for removal at the same moment, except the shared files listed under "What is kept".

Can't sign in?

Email founder@connects.university from the email address on your account, with the subject Delete my Connects account and your username. We will confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, username, photos and education details
  • Your posts, comments, likes, Sparks, poll votes and Glimpses
  • Your messages and connection (Respect) records
  • Files you uploaded (photos, Glimpses, Stories, Study Vault PDFs, portfolio images, teacher verification documents)
  • Your sign-in and email address

What is kept (without your name)

  • Work you share with another person — freelance jobs, proposals, contracts with their messages and delivery files, class assignments and submissions with their submitted files, mentor bookings and reviews — stays for the other person and shows you as "Deleted user". Deleting it would delete their work too.
  • Reports you filed about other members, so our team can finish reviewing them.
  • Reports other members filed about you, with the copy of the reported content saved in the report (for example the text of a reported message, or your name if your profile was reported). Only authorized administrators can read them. We keep them as a safety record for as long as they are needed to handle abuse and repeat offences. To ask us to remove them, email founder@connects.university.
  • Records we must keep for security or payments, if any.

Copies may remain in service-provider backups for a limited period until they are overwritten.
