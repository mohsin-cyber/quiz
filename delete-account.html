Delete your Connects University account

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Open the Connects University app or go to connects.university and sign in.
  2. Open My profile.
  3. Under Profile & account settings, tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Can't sign in?

Email founder@connects.university from the email address on your account, with the subject Delete my Connects account and your username. We will confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, username, photos and education details
  • Your posts, comments, likes, Sparks, poll votes and Glimpses
  • Your messages and connection (Respect) records
  • Files you uploaded (photos, documents, assignment files)
  • Your sign-in and email address

What is kept (without your name)

  • Work you share with another person — freelance jobs, proposals, contracts and their messages, class assignments and submissions, mentor bookings and reviews — stays for the other person and shows you as "Deleted user". Deleting it would delete their work too.
  • Reports you filed about other members, so our team can finish reviewing them.
  • Records we must keep for security or payments, if any.

Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.
