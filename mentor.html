Mentor story

From classroom guidance to meaningful impact

Mohsin Haider Shakeel met Raza Abbas while studying Project Management at Iqra University. What began as a teacher-student connection grew into mentorship grounded in practical advice, professional discipline and purposeful networking.

Raza helped Mohsin connect with the right people, communicate the mission of Connects University more effectively and expand the platform's reach. His guidance continues to support better decisions as the platform grows from an idea into a trusted student community.

As a mentor to Connects University, he brings an industry-informed perspective to a mission focused on education access, credible connections and long-term opportunities for students.
